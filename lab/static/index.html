BASE PAPER

Protein Design by Directed Evolution Guided by Large Language Models

Thanh V. T. Tran and Truong Son Hy · IEEE TEVC, 29(2), 418–428, 2025.

The method combines random and importance masking, ESM-2 mutation proposals, and an Attention1D fitness predictor with elitist population selection.

Read the publication ↗
CURRENT SCOPE

A working local foundation

  • CPU ridge baseline and avGFP experiment tracking.
  • ESM adapter reuses the original Attention1D decoder and importance masker.
  • ESM settings differ from the paper: 128 hidden units, reduced samples, random holdout, local starting sequence, 50/50 masking, two proposals per population member.
  • Independent oracle weights, full benchmark suite, and paper table reproduction remain outstanding.
COMPARISON STRATEGY

Uncertainty plus diversity selection

Optional ESM mode trains three bootstrap heads, ranks candidates by mean + 0.5σ, and adds a normalized minimum Hamming-distance bonus during population selection. Prior work already uses uncertainty-guided active learning in ALDE and co-optimizes fitness with diversity in MODIFY.

Treat this implementation as a comparator, not the project novelty. A possible contribution is calibrated uncertainty on mutation-distance splits in this ESM masked-proposal loop; validate that gap with a broader review and controlled ablations.

REPLICATION CHECKLIST

What counts as reproduced?

  1. Match paper models, training settings, and data splits.
  2. Obtain and verify independent oracle artifacts.
  3. Run repeated seeds and baseline comparisons.
  4. Reproduce reported metrics with uncertainty intervals.

A successful local demonstration is not yet a complete reproduction.
